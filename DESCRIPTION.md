# Slimefun United

A modern, organised, community‑driven continuation of the Slimefun legacy — with full localisation support (WIP), active development, and compatibility with old saves.  
✨ _Magic, technology, automation, reactors, factories… all in one plugin._

---

## 🧪 What is Slimefun United?

Slimefun United brings together the best features from the original Slimefun and its most popular forks, combining them into a single, actively maintained project.  
It offers a **modded‑like experience** on an **unmodded Minecraft server**, adding:

- ⚡ Energy networks
- 🔥 Reactors and generators
- 🏭 Automation systems
- 🧪 Magic altars and runes
- 📦 Item transport systems
- 🛠️ Hundreds of machines, tools, and gadgets
- 🌍 Full translation support
- 🔌 A huge ecosystem of addons

Compatible with worlds previously using:

- The original Slimefun 4
- Gugu Project’s fork
- Other community forks (on request)

Slimefun has existed since **2013**, with **500+ items** and **hundreds of contributors**. Slimefun United continues that legacy with modern standards, frequent updates, and a welcoming community.
